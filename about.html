About Me

Guiding families towards the right health cover

I’m Bibin Babu, Branch Manager at Niva Bupa Health Insurance, based in Thiruvananthapuram. My work is simple to describe: I help families understand health insurance and choose a plan that fits their needs, their budget and their stage of life.

Health insurance can feel complicated, with waiting periods, sub-limits, co-payments and claim procedures. I explain each of these in plain language, so you know exactly what you are buying and what to expect when you need to use it.

Beyond issuing a policy, I stay with you afterwards, helping with documentation, cashless hospitalisation, claims and renewals. A policy proves its worth only when it works at the moment of need, and that is what I work towards for every family.
