Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This website, bibinbabu.in, is the professional portfolio of Bibin Babu, Branch Manager at Niva Bupa Health Insurance, Thiruvananthapuram. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information you share

The enquiry form on the Contact page does not send your details to any server. When you submit it, your name, phone number, city, plan interest and message are placed into a pre-filled WhatsApp message or email, which opens on your device. You choose whether to send it. This website does not store your enquiry.

When you contact me

If you send a message on WhatsApp, call, or email, I will use the details you share only to respond to your enquiry and give you health insurance guidance. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use accounts, and does not set advertising cookies of its own.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts is used to display the site’s typefaces.
  • Google Maps is used to show the office location on the Contact page. Opening it is subject to Google’s privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp opens only when you tap a WhatsApp button.

Sharing of information

Your details are not sold. They are shared only where needed to assist with an insurance enquiry you have asked about, or where required by law.

Your choices

You can ask me to delete any conversation or details you have shared by writing to Bibin.Babu@nivabupa.com.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
