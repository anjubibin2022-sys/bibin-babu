ReAssure 2.0

Nothing Seems Impossible

Best for: families wanting long-term, flexible cover

A comprehensive plan designed so your cover is not used up: it can be restored after a claim, and unused cover is carried forward.

  • Lock the Clock: pay as per entry age until you claim
  • ReAssure Forever: unlimited times after the 1st claim
  • Booster+: carry forward unused sum insured
  • Safeguard+: non-payables covered
  • Live Healthy: up to 30% renewal discount on step count
Benefits explained
Lock the Clock
Your premium stays based on the age you joined, until you make a claim. You save as you age.
ReAssure Forever
Switches on after your first claim and restores your sum insured, unlimited times and forever.
Booster+
Unused sum insured is carried forward, so you don’t lose what you don’t use.
Safeguard+
Covers non-payable items that are normally paid out of pocket.
Live Healthy
Up to 30% discount on renewal premium, based on your step count.

Aspire

Start Young

Best for: young adults, newlyweds and couples planning a family

A plan that rewards you for starting young, with benefits around maternity, fertility and long-term cover.

  • M-iracle: maternity, IVF, adoption, surrogacy
  • Lock the Clock+ and Booster+ (carry forward up to 10X)
  • Cash-Bag: cashback on every claim-free year
  • Fast Forward and Borderless (treatment anywhere in the world)
Benefits explained
M-iracle
Covers expenses around maternity, IVF, adoption, surrogacy and more. The balance sum insured can be carried forward up to 10X.
Lock the Clock+
Pay as per your entry age until you claim. An M-iracle claim does not affect the age lock.
Booster+
Unused sum insured is carried forward, up to 10X.
Cash-Bag
Cashback on every claim-free year, usable towards premiums, deductible, co-payment and OPD.
Fast Forward
Don’t wait for what you’ve paid: bring the future base sum insured and M-iracle sum insured forward.
Borderless
Get treatment anywhere in the world.

Senior First

A Promise of Love

Best for: senior citizens and parents

Health protection designed with senior citizens in mind, with fewer restrictions on common conditions and a yearly health check-up.

  • ReAssure Benefit: unlimited, after the 1st claim
  • Day 1 Health Check-up
  • No sub-limits on common conditions like cataract and joint replacement
  • No mandatory pre-issuance medical tests
  • Safeguard (add-on) and Annual Aggregate Deductible option
Benefits explained
ReAssure Benefit
Triggers after the first claim and is unlimited. Each claim is equal to the base sum insured.
Day 1 Health Check-up
Annual health check-up available from day 1.
No sub-limits
No sub-limits on common conditions such as cataract, joint replacements, cancer and more.
Safeguard (add-on)
Go truly cashless with cover even for non-payable items like gloves and masks.
Annual Aggregate Deductible
If a deductible is opted, co-payment will not apply.

Health Recharge

Super Top-up Plan

Best for: people who already have a base policy and want higher cover affordably

A super top-up that adds cover on top of your existing policy. It starts paying once your claims in a year go beyond the deductible you choose.

  • Cover up to ₹95 lacs
  • Pre & post hospitalisation and day care covered
  • Unlimited tele / online consultations
  • Easy convertibility to an indemnity policy after 5 years
Benefits explained
Super top-up
Works above the selected deductible, adding extra protection for large medical bills.
Coverage
Up to ₹95 lacs.
Pre & post hospitalisation
Medical expenses before and after hospitalisation covered up to the sum insured.
e-Consultation
Unlimited tele / online consultations.
Pharmacy & diagnostics
Available through empanelled service providers.
Day care treatments
Covered for all day care treatments up to the sum insured.
Easy convertibility
Convert to an indemnity policy (without deductible) after 5 years.
